Welcome to Ganze Vocational Training Centre

Ganze Vocational Training Centre provides quality technical and vocational training aimed at equipping youth and adults with practical skills for employment and self-reliance.

About Us

Ganze Vocational Training Centre is committed to offering competency-based training in various technical fields including Information Technology, Fashion Design, Plumbing, Electrical Installation, Carpentry and Joinery, Hair Dressing, and Masonry.
